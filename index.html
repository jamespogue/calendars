Calendars

Subscribe to any of these calendars and they'll stay up to date automatically in your calendar app. Subscribing is different from importing: an imported file never receives changes.

How to subscribe
  • iPhone, iPad, or Mac: tap Apple / Outlook and confirm Subscribe.
  • Google Calendar: click Google Calendar and confirm Add. On a phone, do this once from a computer; the calendar then syncs to the Google Calendar app.
  • Outlook (desktop): tap Apple / Outlook, or copy the link and use Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  • Anything else: copy the link and look for Add calendar from URL or Subscribe.

Calendar apps check for updates on their own schedule (Google can take up to a day), so changes may not appear right away.
